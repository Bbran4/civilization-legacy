# Province appearance

In the Provinces properties, Province view now offers Colored provinces and
Borders only — no province fill. The second option keeps terrain shading and
province borders without the random land-province fill. Coastal water colors and
impassable land remain visible. Selection highlighting remains an editing aid.
Play map exposes the same appearance choice next to its Provinces switch.
This is presentation only and does not change saved IDs or source fields.
